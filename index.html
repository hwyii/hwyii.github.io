Weiyi He / 贺维易

Ph.D. candidate at Michigan State University

Hello! I’m Weiyi He, a third-year Ph.D. student at Michigan State University (MSU), dual-majoring in Statistics and Computer Science. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yue Xing and Prof. Jiliang Tang. I received my B.S. in Statistics from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I received the Outstanding Graduate Award.

My research focuses on Trustworthy AI, particularly LLM robustness and Adversarial Training. I am interested in developing statistical tools to understand modern AI systems and principled methods to improve their reliability. I was an Applied Scientist Intern at [Amazon AGI](https://www.amazon.science/), where I worked with Giuseppe Vietri, Dionysis Manousakas, and Dmitriy Bespalov on understanding and evaluating LLM robustness to input and parameter perturbations.

Bold indicates first or co-first authorship._
