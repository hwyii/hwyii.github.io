Hello! I'm **Weiyi He**, a third-year Ph.D. student at <a class="org-link" href="https://msu.edu"><img class="org-logo org-logo-seal" src="{{ '/assets/img/venues/msu.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="">Michigan State University (MSU)</a>, dual-majoring in Statistics and Computer Science. I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Yue Xing](https://sites.google.com/site/xingyuecuhk/) and [Prof. Jiliang Tang](https://www.cse.msu.edu/~tangjili/index.html). I received my B.S. in Statistics from the <a class="org-link" href="https://en.ustc.edu.cn/"><img class="org-logo org-logo-seal" src="{{ '/assets/img/venues/ustc.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="">University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)</a>.

My research focuses on **Trustworthy AI**, particularly **LLM robustness** and **Adversarial Training**. I am interested in developing statistical tools to understand modern AI systems and principled methods to improve their reliability. I was an Applied Scientist Intern at <a class="org-link" href="https://www.amazon.science/"><img class="org-logo org-logo-amazon" src="{{ '/assets/img/company/amazon.svg' | relative_url }}" alt=""><strong>Amazon AGI</strong></a>, where I worked with [Giuseppe Vietri](https://www.giuseppevietri.com/), [Dionysis Manousakas](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~dm754/), and [Dmitriy Bespalov](https://github.com/dbespalov) on understanding and evaluating LLM robustness to input and parameter perturbations.

**\*Bold** indicates first or co-first authorship.
